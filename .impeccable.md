## Design Context

### Users
Visitors exploring a cinematic, first-person sci-fi space station in a browser. The creator requested an immense, immersive experience inspired by Port FreeCommerce in the Murderbot books, with creative freedom.

### Brand Personality
Monumental, inhabited, quietly unsettling. Evoke wonder, scale, and the anonymity of a vast commercial port.

### Aesthetic Direction
Industrial science fiction: slate steel, worn concrete, warm amber navigation, utilitarian signage, layered architecture, and distant planetary light. An original unofficial fan interpretation, with original geometry and copy. Full-viewport 3D scenery, restrained editorial typography, and a minimal diegetic exploration HUD.

### Design Principles
- The station is the main event: render real geometry immediately.
- Make size tangible through repeated structural bays, balconies, transit movement, and human silhouettes.
- Offer an easy cinematic arrival and immediate first-person exploration.
- Keep navigation, sound, motion, and touch controls accessible and optional.
- Favor procedural reusable geometry over asset dependencies.
